MDS Vatican Cozy Retreat Walk to Metro & Vatican

56 m² for families, two minutes from Cipro metro station and seven from the Vatican Museums.

Family apartment · Via Angelo Emo 5, 00136 Roma

  • 5 guests
  • 1 bedroom
  • 1 bathroom
  • 56 m²

The Vatican Cozy Retreat is a 56 m² apartment on Via Angelo Emo, in the Trionfale district, in a period building with a grand staircase and lift. It sleeps up to 5 people and is designed for families and small groups.

The layout is bright and open-plan: the bedroom, separated by a glass partition, has a double bed and a single bed, while the living area has a wooden table with benches, a sofa and a TV. The kitchen comes complete with oven, microwave and toaster, and we can provide a cot on request.

It is one of the handiest locations for the Vatican: Cipro metro (line A) is two minutes away, the Vatican Museums entrance about seven, and the Trionfale Market, one of Rome's largest covered markets, four.
