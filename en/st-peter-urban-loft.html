MDS St Peter Urban Loft Renovated Studio

A freshly renovated studio for two, bright and quiet.

Renovated studio · Via Gregorio VII 242, 00165 Roma

  • 2 guests
  • 1 bedroom
  • 1 bathroom
  • 30 m²

The St Peter Urban Loft is a recently renovated 30 m² studio on Via Gregorio VII, the long avenue that runs down towards St Peter's from the Aurelio district. White walls, pale wood and sage-green fabrics: a calm, uncluttered space to sleep well and work in peace.

In a single open room you'll find the double bed, a sofa, a table that doubles as a desk and the kitchenette with a moka pot. The brand-new bathroom has a walk-in shower and a countertop basin.

It's the right choice for couples, solo travellers and anyone in Rome on business: the area is residential and well served by buses, with Roma San Pietro station less than twenty minutes' walk away.
