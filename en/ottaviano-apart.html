MDS Ottaviano Apart 2 Bedrooms Walk to St Peter

85 m² of light-filled space in the heart of Prati, a ten-minute walk from St Peter's Square.

Two-bedroom apartment · Via Ottaviano 25, 00192 Roma

  • 6 guests
  • 2 bedrooms
  • 1 bathroom
  • 85 m²

The Ottaviano Apart is an 85 m² apartment at number 25 Via Ottaviano, the street that links the metro to St Peter's colonnade. Two bedrooms, a living room with sofa and dining table, a fully equipped kitchen and a bathroom with shower: just the right amount of space for a family or group of friends of up to 6 people.

The colourful walls (petrol blue, orange, sky blue) and the light from the street-facing windows make the flat welcoming in every season. The kitchen has an oven, microwave, dishwasher, coffee machine and kettle, with coffee, tea and olive oil already in the cupboard.

Step outside and you're in Prati, the most convenient neighbourhood for visiting the Vatican: Piazza Risorgimento is just around the corner, the shops of Via Cola di Rienzo are a few minutes away, and Ottaviano metro station (Line A) is less than 300 metres from the door.
