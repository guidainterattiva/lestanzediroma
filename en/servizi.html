Services

Services for our guests

Beyond your accommodation, we'll help you plan your trip: transfers, tours, car hire and tips on where to eat. All on request, with a single point of contact.

Fiumicino · Ciampino · Termini · Tiburtina

Private drivers and transfers

We'll meet you at the airport or station with a licensed private driver and take you back when it's time to leave. Just tell us your arrival time and how much luggage you have, and we'll arrange a transfer to suit you.

Request a transfer

Private tours in several languages

Golf cart tours

Thanks to our partnership with Exploring Rome, you can discover the city aboard a golf cart with a driver-guide: a relaxed way to see lots of sights in just a few hours, ideal with children or for anyone who can't walk far.

Discover Exploring Rome
